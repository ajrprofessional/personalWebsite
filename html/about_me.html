About Me

I was born in Lima, Peru in the year 2000 and raised predominantly in Naples, Florida. My fascination with technology began in 6th grade, with my dream job becoming the CEO of Apple. With time, I learned to manage my goals and set more attainable goals to build my competency. In high school, I was enrolled in an engineering fundamentals class, which was the first time that engineering electives were offered at the school. During my senior year, I was enrolled in a digital electronics class, and learned about Boolean algebra, logic gates, programming using breadboards, etc.
